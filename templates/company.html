About this record

TRC here measures nonfatal recordable injuries and illnesses per 100 full-time-equivalent workers; DART covers cases involving days away, restricted duty or transfer; fatalities are shown separately. These are case rates, not the percentage of workers injured. Smaller sites (under 250 workers) count toward the totals but do not get their own page; search them from the home page. A company-wide rate hides variation — a warehouse and a head office in the same filing pool look very different.
