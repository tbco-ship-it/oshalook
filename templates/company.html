About this record

Sites are grouped by the parent company name employers typed into OSHA's system, so subsidiaries filed under another name are not included and the same company can appear twice under variant names. Smaller sites (under 250 workers) count toward the totals but do not get their own page; search them from the home page. A company-wide rate hides variation — a warehouse and a head office in the same filing pool look very different.
