About InjuryRate

OSHA publishes the injury summaries employers file, but as a 380,000-row spreadsheet with no benchmarks. InjuryRate turns each filing into a page — rate, cases, deaths, three-year trend — next to the median for the employer's industry, and rolls sites up to the company, so a job candidate, a contractor's safety manager or a reporter can answer "is this employer unusually dangerous?" in one look.

Supported by ads. Nothing you search is sent anywhere but this site's own static files. Corrections: contact.
