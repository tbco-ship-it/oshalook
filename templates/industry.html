Using the benchmark

Compare a site's TRC with the median and upper quartile above, not with the all-industry number: a 3.0 reads very differently in meatpacking and in software. These benchmarks describe the reporting establishments included here, not all U.S. employers. The median gives each establishment equal weight; BLS estimates use survey weights and pooled cases and hours, so the figures are not directly interchangeable and differences can go in either direction.
