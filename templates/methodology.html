Data & method

Source. OSHA Injury Tracking Application, Form 300A summary data for calendar years {{ source.years|join(', ') }}, downloaded {{ source.fetched }}. Public domain. {{ '{:,}'.format(n_filings) }} establishments filed for {{ latest }}.

Rates. TRC = (days-away + restricted/transfer + other recordable cases) × 200,000 ÷ total hours worked; DART = (days-away + restricted/transfer) × 200,000 ÷ hours. Computed per establishment and per year exactly as OSHA and BLS define them.

Validity filter. A filing gets no rate when hours per employee fall outside 300–4,000 or recordable cases exceed twice the headcount — the typo patterns that dominate ITA data errors. Those sites still appear in search with their raw counts.

Benchmarks. Median and quartiles of establishment TRC/DART within the same 6-digit NAICS code, from these filings, when at least 20 sites filed; otherwise the 4-, 3- or 2-digit NAICS group. "Hours-weighted" pools all cases and hours in the group. Because only employers required to report are included, benchmarks sit above BLS survey rates for the same industry.

Companies. Sites are grouped by the parent company name as filed (normalized for punctuation and suffixes such as Inc. or LLC). Company pages need 5 or more filing sites; establishment pages need 250 or more average employees. Everything else is searchable.

Refresh. OSHA posts each year's filings after the March 2 deadline and updates them through the year; the site is rebuilt when a new file lands.
