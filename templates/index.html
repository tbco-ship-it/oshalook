What the number means

The Total Recordable Case rate (TRC) is injuries and illnesses serious enough to log on OSHA Form 300 — medical treatment beyond first aid, lost or restricted work, loss of consciousness — per 200,000 hours worked, which is 100 full-time employees for a year. A TRC of 4.0 means four such cases per hundred workers. DART counts only the cases that cost days away from work or restricted duty. Where it comes from and what it misses

Employers with 20 or more workers in high-hazard industries and 250 or more anywhere must submit their annual 300A summary to OSHA electronically; OSHA publishes it. It is self-reported, un-audited, and under-recording is a known problem, so a zero is not proof of safety. Inspections and citations are a separate record — how to pull that too.
